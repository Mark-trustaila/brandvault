The problem

Seventy trademarks across twelve countries, and only one of you.

If you're the lawyer responsible for your company's trademarks, you know how this goes. The renewal decision

Is this mark worth renewing? BrandVault gathers the evidence; you make the decision.

A trademark renewal costs money in every country you hold it, and most portfolios carry marks no longer used in territories the business left years ago. They get renewed each cycle anyway, because the deadline arrived and there was no evidence on hand to justify saying no.

BrandVault changes what's on hand. For each mark in each territory, it gathers the commercial signals (is the brand searched for, sold under, present online in that market?) and scores them into a recommendation with reasons: renew, let lapse, or investigate. Your budget goes to the marks that protect real business, and the rest stops costing you money.

Under the hood

Not a chat window on a spreadsheet

Legacy tools are bolting chatbots onto twenty-year-old databases. BrandVault is built the other way round: AI baked in, not sprinkled on top, every workflow from docketing to drafting running through the same agentic core. The app, Slack and email are just ways in.

The data

BrandVault holds 10 million trademark records from the registries that matter most (UKIPO, EUIPO, USPTO and WIPO), drawn from their official bulk data. When Bree answers, the answer is grounded in the official record and traceable to it.

The harness

Bree runs a leading AI model inside BrandVault's own UK IP harness. The model reads, drafts and explains, but never guesses at law or arithmetic: deadlines come from a deterministic rules engine built per jurisdiction. The model can't miscalculate a renewal date, because it doesn't calculate them.

The rules

Independent legal rights are never merged: post-Brexit UK and EU parallel registrations are tracked as the separate rights they are. Where a jurisdiction's rules haven't been verified, the deadline is flagged for confirmation, not guessed. Anything consequential requires your approval, and is logged.

The value is in the detailed trademark knowledge embedded in the agentic layer, and in the constantly refreshed official records beneath it.

FAQ

Questions you may have

Where does my trial portfolio come from?

From official registry records. BrandVault holds bulk trademark data from the registries we cover, and matches marks registered to the exact name you enter. Your first experience of BrandVault is a working portfolio, not an empty screen and an import wizard.

What if some of my marks are missing from the trial?

Trial portfolios match only the name you entered, so marks held by subsidiaries, group companies, previous names or individuals won't appear until we curate the full portfolio as part of onboarding. Gaps in the trial are expected, and telling us about them is the fastest way to the complete picture.

What does BrandVault actually do without me?

Monitoring, deadline calculation, alerts, digests and the preparation of renewal recommendations all happen automatically. Anything consequential (a filing, a renewal payment, a response to a registry) is prepared for your approval and never submitted without it.

Who are the lawyers behind escalation?

AiLA's own legal team: specialists in trademark prosecution and contentious registry work, working with the AI rather than instead of it. Your portfolio is already in the system, so an escalated matter starts with full context on day one, with no briefing calls and no catch-up fees.

How is this different from docketing software?

Docketing software tracks dates, leaves the work to you, and leaves you entirely alone when a dispute arrives. BrandVault does the routine work, brings you the decisions with the evidence attached, and handles the contentious matters when they come.

What's Bree?

BrandVault's AI trademark paralegal, living in Slack. Bree sends deadline alerts and weekly digests, and answers questions (what's due this quarter, the status of a mark, what needs attention) without you opening the app.

Can you tell me whether to renew a mark?

We tell you what the evidence says and make a recommendation. The decision stays yours: it's your legal right and your budget. What we remove is the guesswork.

Do you cover registered designs?

Yes. Designs and trademarks are both brand equity, and portfolios usually contain both, so BrandVault tracks registered designs with the same deadline engine and the same evidence-based renewal decisions, in the same portfolio view.

Which countries do you cover?

Deadline rules are currently built for seven registries including UKIPO, EUIPO, WIPO, and USPTO, with more added as customers need them. Where we haven't verified a jurisdiction's rules, we say so and flag the deadline for manual confirmation rather than guessing.

What happens to UK and EU marks after Brexit?

They're treated as separate legal rights, because they are. Comparable UK marks created from EU registrations are tracked distinctly, with their own deadlines and scope. Tools that merge them get this wrong.
